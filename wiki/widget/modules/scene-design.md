# Scene Design — "Plumber's Run"

The visual identity of scene mode: what it is, why it maps well to the
phases, and the design decisions that make the four states *consistent*
and *engaging* (this page started as a brainstorm on the "idle too busy /
working too flat" problems — the recommended change set has since been
**implemented**; §3–§4 document the reasoning, §5 is the as-built design).

**Pipeline (current):** the scene is no longer drawn procedurally at runtime.
`shared/scene-assets/render_scene.py` rasterises each state to a **static
bitmap** (one frozen frame per state) for both targets — see
[`shared/scene-assets/README.md`](../../../shared/scene-assets/README.md).
The widget (`widget/src/main.rs` → `scene_assets` module) and the device
(`esp32c6_480x480/firmware/plumber_scene.h`, generated by
`shared/scene-assets/render_scene.py`) blit those pictures; the
per-frame animation in §2/§5 below is what the working/prompting frames
were *pinned from*. Idle is **scene-only** (no plumber); down shows a
Piranha Plant in the middle of the scene. (The standalone **night** picture
was removed entirely; there are exactly four states.)

Original concept sheets: `shared/scene-assets/scene_design_sheet.png`.
Golden reference renders: `widget/tests/snapshots/scene_*.png`.

---

## 1. The concept

A tiny original **plumber** (deliberately *not* Nintendo IP) living in a
classic 8-bit platformer level:

```
   sun      clouds
   🌤   ☁        ☁
            ?  ?
   ▓▓▓▓  ▓▓
   pipe  bricks        ground: grass over dirt
```

- **Grid**: 80×80 logical cells — the *same
  pre-rendered bitmap* fills the card band and the full 480×480 device panel
  (full-bleed square, no letterbox), so the widget and device always show the
  same picture.
- **No assets**: everything is code (a `Painter` that converts grid
  coordinates to screen quads, rounded/clipped per band).
- **Animation**: one `frame: u64` counter injected by the UI (~8 fps,
  wall-clock derived), making the module pure and golden-testable. The
  scene repeats exactly every `LOOP = 960` frames (2 min at 8 fps) — the
  LCM of the base 8-frame character cycle and the slower event sub-loops
  (see §5).
- **Multi-server**: each server is one full *band* (its own level strip),
  stacked top-to-bottom — "combined activity": a busy server sprints (on the
  ground), an idle one shows the calm level only (no plumber), a down one
  shows the Piranha Plant.

**Why a platformer level is a good status metaphor**

| Property | Why it fits a GPU/inference buddy |
|---|---|
| One recognizable subject | The eye tracks *the character's motion* = the server's activity |
| A fixed "stage" (ground, pipe, blocks) | Stable reference frame — changes are always meaningful |
| Motion vs. stillness | Sprint = tokens flowing; standing = no load |
| Small props (coins, ? blocks) | Natural vocabulary for "work products" / "pending work" |

## 2. State → animation mapping (as-built)

All four states share the **same day level** (the earlier day/night split
was rejected: the user wants working and idle to read as the *same place*).
The states differ only in **tempo** — which elements move and how fast:

| Phase | Character | World | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generating** | Sprint (4-substep leg/arm cycle, body bob) | ground ticks scroll left; far cloud sweeps across the sky; **no coin row** | Busy |
| **Prompting** | **Same picture as Generating** (reused; no distinct art), **no coin row** | same (sprint, on the ground) | **Busy + pending** |
| **Idle** | — (no plumber, scene only) | everything frozen: static clouds, **no coin row** | Resting |
| **Down** | Piranha Plant in the middle of the scene (red spotted head, open fanged maw, green stalk) — no plumber, no coins | same as idle | Alert |

Design rules that were settled earlier and must not regress:

1. **One character, one stage.** The character is the only "actor"; props
   are set dressing.
2. **Day-only.** No moon/stars anywhere in any state.
3. **State must be readable in 1 second** at 320px width from across a
   desk: tempo (sprint+scroll vs. frozen) is the primary signal; the badge
   text is secondary.
4. **The frame loop must be seamless** (`frame N+LOOP == frame N`,
   `LOOP = 960`) so goldens stay deterministic — enforced by
   `frames_are_deterministic_and_loop`.
5. **The character must stay clear of the props** (no overlap with the
   pipe at grid x≈7–17, and nothing sits under the badge area on the right
   of each band).

## 3. The two problems (why the redesign happened)

### P1 — Idle looked like it had too much action

The idle band is *supposed* to be the quiet state, but the current
composition still runs the **full prop animation loop**: the two ? blocks
pulse their "?" glyph every 2 frames, the five coins blink alternately,
and the clouds drift. A vision review of the golden ranks the **blinking
coins and pulsing blocks as the #1 and #3 eye-catchers** — *above* the
character. So an idle server looks busy in its background even though the
hero is asleep. The nap reads as "standing next to a slot machine".

**Root cause:** one shared `level()` painter animates everything for every
phase; there is no notion of "calm mode".

### P2 — Working looked monotonous

The sprinting character is nice, but the level itself never changes:
same clouds, same pulsing pattern, same bobbing coins, forever. The eye
quickly habituates — after a few seconds the band is just "noise". A
status widget you glance at *often* needs something that **happens**
occasionally, not just something that vibrates continuously.

**Root cause:** all animation is *periodic vibration* (blink/pulse/drift)
with no **events** (a coin getting collected, a block being bumped, a
jump). Vibration decays perceptually fast; events stay interesting.

## 4. Brainstorm — options (what was considered)

### A. Calm the idle state

| # | Option | What changes | Pros | Cons |
|---|--------|-------------|------|------|
| A1 | **Quiet props** | ? blocks & coins freeze (static colors) while idle; only the Z's drift | Kills the #1 and #3 eye-catchers in idle; 1-line switch | Idle band goes near-static (may look *dead*) |
| A2 | **Breathing hero** | Idle character gets a 2px "breath" bob + occasional eye blink (every ~2s) | Character stays the alive part; nap reads instantly | Needs a slow timer derived from the 8fps frame (fine: every 16th frame) |
| A3 | **Slow clouds only** | All prop animation off, but clouds drift on a 16-frame cycle (twice as slow) | One gentle ambient motion; feels like a quiet afternoon | Still two moving things (clouds + Z's) |

**Recommendation: A1 + A2** (freeze the props, keep the hero alive). The
state then has exactly **one** focus: the sleeping character with drifting
Z's. A3 is a good fallback if A1+A2 looks too static on the desk.

### B. Add events to the working state

| # | Option | What changes | Pros | Cons |
|---|--------|-------------|------|------|
| B1 | **Parallax scroll** | Ground ticks + clouds scroll left continuously, faster at higher frames; props pass by and wrap | Instantly sells forward motion; level stops looking frozen | Must scroll *everything* that should move (prop wrap-around logic) |
| B2 | **Coin run** | A line of coins marches toward the character, the nearest pops (flashes white, vanishes), a new one spawns at the end — a loop that reads as "collecting work" | A discrete *event* every ~1s; reads as progress/output | More state in the painter (which coin is popping) |
| B3 | **? block bump** | When the character reaches under a ? block (position in the loop), the block bobs up and a coin pops out | Classic feel; ties the character to the props | Character would need to actually *travel* (currently runs in place) — bigger change |
| B4 | **Speed tied to tok/s** | Pass the latest `tok/s` into the scene; sprint cadence + scroll speed scale with load | The scene encodes *data*, not just phase — most informative option | Couples scene to metric timing; goldens need a fixed tok/s seed; easy to overdo (flicker) |
| B5 | **Bird/prop cameo** | A bird flies across every ~4s (32 frames), or a small flag/goomba appears occasionally | Cheap episodic variety; zero new mechanics | Random-looking motion can re-trigger the "too much action" complaint if not kept rare |

**Recommendation: B1 + B2** (scroll the world, collect coins). Together
they give: continuous motion (scroll) + a discrete event roughly once a
second (coin pop). B4 is a strong *phase-2* idea once B1 exists (scroll
speed = f(tok/s)); B3 needs character travel and is the biggest lift; B5
is a nice-to-have garnish only if B1+B2 still feels flat.

### C. Consistency rules (apply to both)

1. **Attention budget per state** — cap the number of *animated* elements:
   - Idle: ≤ 2 (Z's + breathing; clouds static)
   - Down: same as idle (the tick is static)
   - Prompting: same picture as generating (reused; no added sparkle)
   - Generating: character + scroll + coin pop (+ prop pulse may drop out
     entirely — the event replaces it)
2. **Same stage, same positions.** Pipe, blocks, ground, sun, clouds keep
   identical anchors across all states (already true; keep it). A state
   should change *behavior*, not *scenery*.
3. **One hero, one focus.** Any new element must not out-compete the
   character for attention. New motion goes into the background (slow) or
   near the character (coins), never large/central.
4. **Vibrate less, happen more.** Prefer low-frequency events (1–2 Hz at
   most) over constant 4 Hz blinking. The 8 fps loop is long enough for a
   "something happened" beat every 8–32 frames.
5. **Seamless loop for goldens**; the base character cycle stays 8 frames,
   and event timings may use longer sub-loops (e.g. `frame % 32`,
   `frame % 192`) as long as every period divides the full `LOOP` —
   the unit test pins specific frames, and
   `frames_are_deterministic_and_loop` asserts `frame N+LOOP == frame N`
   for both tempos.

## 5. The as-built design (implemented)

**Idle / Down — the calm tempo** (A1, as-built in the bitmaps):

- All props **frozen**: clouds sit still, and there is **no coin row in any
  state** — the coins were removed from the level entirely (they were the
  "work" signal, but the user asked them out).
- **No character** in idle or down: the calm level itself; the plumber
  appears only in working/prompting.
- Down = calm level + a **Piranha Plant roaring up in the middle of the
  scene** (classic SMB Pakun Flower: red spotted head, open fanged maw,
  green stalk). No plumber, no coins.

**Generating / Prompting — the working tempo** (B1, as-built):

- **Scroll**: the ground dirt ticks (period 6 cells) stream left under the
  hero, selling forward motion.
- **Cloud sweep**: one cloud stays static (the shared anchor); the other
  sweeps right-to-left across the sky on a 192-frame (~24 s) period — a
  rare slow event, not a vibrate.
- **No coin run** — the coins were removed from the level (the B2 beat is
  gone).
- The plumber sprints **on the ground** (no jumping — the user asked the
  prompting state to stop hopping); prompting now reuses the **same picture
  as working** (the distinct thinking sparkle was dropped).
  The sprite is a 16×20 native authoring (cap with emblem, mustache,
  overall pocket + buttons, two distinct boots) blitted at 3 px/cell so it
  reads large without pixelating further.

**Timing invariants**: every sub-loop divides `LOOP = 960`
(8 | 960, 6 | 960, 32 | 960, 40 | 960, 192 | 960), so the scene is
bit-identical every 2 minutes and goldens (which pin frame 3) stay
stable.

**What was deliberately not done** (kept as future work):

- B4 — tok/s-driven scroll speed (would make the scene encode live data,
  not just phase; needs a metric input + fixed seed for goldens).
- B5 — a rare bird/prop cameo, only if the working tempo still feels flat
  after B1+B2 in the wild.
- B3 — ? block bump (needs the character to actually travel through the
  level; biggest structural change).

## 6. Future ideas (backlog)

- B4 — tok/s-driven scroll + sprint cadence (scene encodes live load).
- B5 — rare bird/prop cameo for episodic variety.
- B3 — character actually travels through the level, bumping ? blocks.
- Per-band pacing differences for multiple servers (each server's band
  scrolling at its own tok/s).
- Idle: a very slow cloud drift if the frozen tempo ever looks too dead
  on the desk (the A3 fallback from §4).
